Schola Romana

A Course in the History of Rome

Forty lessons in order, from the founding of the City in 753 BC to the last stand at Constantinople in AD 1453. Every lesson has its map, its chronicle, and the ancient writers in their own words, and every age ends in a trial that raises your rank on the cursus honorum.

The course is free.
No ads and no accounts; your place and your rank are kept in this browser. If it has been worth your time, consider supporting our work.
