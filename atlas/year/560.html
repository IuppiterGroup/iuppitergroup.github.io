    The chronicle of these years

    • Chenla Overruns Funanc. A.D. 550, Ba Phnom, Cambodia. Chenla, a vassal kingdom of Khmer speakers on the middle Mekong, turned on its overlord. Under Bhavavarman and his kinsman Chitrasena its armies pressed south and took the old royal hill of Ba Phnom, and the Funan court withdrew toward the coast. Chinese annals record that Chenla swallowed Funan, and with it the delta plains and the Indian learning of its court.
    • Bumin Rises Against the RouranA.D. 552, Altai Mountains, Mongolia. The Türks, ironworkers in the service of the Rouran, were refused a royal bride by the khagan Anagui, who called their chief Bumin a mere blacksmith slave. Bumin allied with Western Wei, routed the Rouran, drove Anagui to suicide, and took the title Il Qaghan. Within a generation his people ruled from Manchuria to the Black Sea.
    • The Turks and Persians Destroy the Hephthalitesc. A.D. 557, Bukhara, Uzbekistan. Khosrow I of Persia allied with Istami, yabghu of the newly risen Turks, and the two powers closed on the Hephthalites from south and north. Near Bukhara the Turks shattered the Hephthalite host under its king Ghadfar, and the realm of the White Huns was divided between the victors along the Oxus. Sogdia passed under the overlordship of the Turkic khaganate.
    • The Lombards Invade ItalyA.D. 568, Forum Iulii, Italy. Alboin, king of the Lombards, who had destroyed the Gepids with the Avars and made a drinking cup of the skull of their king Cunimund, led his people, with Saxons and others, out of Pannonia over the Julian Alps. Italy, exhausted by the Gothic war and plague, fell piece by piece, and the Lombard kingdom ruled from Pavia for two centuries.
    • The Year of the Elephantc. A.D. 570, Mecca, Arabia. Abraha, the Aksumite ruler of Yemen, marched on Mecca, it was said, to destroy the Kaaba and make his own cathedral at Sana'a the goal of Arab pilgrimage, bringing with him an elephant. His army was turned back, and Muslim tradition holds that in that year Muhammad was born to the clan of Hashim of the tribe of Quraysh.
