    The chronicle of these years

    • The Fall of KabulA.D. 2021, Kabul, Afghanistan. As the last American troops withdrew under a bargain struck with the Taliban, the Afghan army collapsed province by province, and on 15 August 2021 President Ashraf Ghani fled and the Taliban entered Kabul. Thousands besieged the airport, some clinging to departing aircraft. The Islamic Emirate was restored, and girls were again barred from secondary school.
    • The Coup of 2021A.D. 2021, Naypyidaw, Myanmar. On 1 February 2021, as the newly elected parliament was to meet, General Min Aung Hlaing arrested Aung San Suu Kyi and seized power, alleging fraud in the vote her party had won. Mass protests were met with gunfire; the deposed lawmakers formed a National Unity Government, and its People's Defence Forces joined the ethnic armies in a civil war across the country.
    • Russia Invades UkraineA.D. 2022, Hostomel, Ukraine. Before dawn on 24 February 2022 Vladimir Putin launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine from Russia, Belarus, and the Crimea, the largest war in Europe since 1945. Russian airborne troops landed at Hostomel airfield and columns drove on Kyiv, but President Zelensky stayed in his capital and the Ukrainians stopped the assault; by early April the Russians had withdrawn from the north.
    • War in SudanA.D. 2023, Khartoum, Sudan. General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan's army and the Rapid Support Forces of Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo, called Hemedti, had overthrown Omar al-Bashir together in 2019 and the civilian government in 2021. On 15 April 2023 they turned on each other in the streets of Khartoum. The war drove over ten million people from their homes, the greatest displacement in the world, and the RSF renewed the massacres of Darfur.
    • The Seventh of OctoberA.D. 2023, Re'im, Israel. On 7 October 2023 Hamas fighters broke through the fence around Gaza and attacked Israeli villages, army posts, and a music festival near Re'im, killing about 1,200 people, most of them civilians, and carrying some 250 hostages into Gaza. Israel answered with a war of bombardment and invasion that by the end of 2024 had killed over forty thousand people in Gaza by its health ministry's count and laid much of the strip in ruins.
    • Operation 1027A.D. 2023, Laukkai, Shan State. On 27 October 2023 three ethnic armies of northern Shan State, the Three Brotherhood Alliance, opened a joint offensive against the junta, overrunning hundreds of outposts and taking the border town of Laukkai in January 2024. In August 2024 the Kokang army captured Lashio, seat of the army's Northeastern Command, one of the gravest defeats the Burmese military had ever suffered.
    • The Fall of AssadA.D. 2024, Damascus, Syria. At the end of November 2024 the rebels of Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham, led by Ahmed al-Sharaa, called Abu Mohammad al-Jolani, swept out of Idlib and took Aleppo in days, while the regime's army melted away. Hama and Homs fell, and on 8 December Bashar al-Assad fled to Moscow as the rebels entered Damascus. Fifty-four years of rule by the Assad family were over.
