    Kingdom of Hawaï'i

    1791 to 1894. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1890; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom united by Kamehameha I, who conquered the islands with the help of European arms between 1795 and 1810, and ruled by his dynasty and its successors from Lahaina and Honolulu. Its kings embraced Christianity and a constitution, and sugar planters of American birth grew powerful, until they overthrew Queen Liliʻuokalani in 1893.

    Government
    Monarchy (constitutional from 1840)
    Faith
    Hawaiian religion until 1819; Protestant Christianity thereafter
    Capitals
    Waikīkī (1804 to 1812); Kailua-Kona (1812 to 1820); Lahaina (1820 to 1845); Honolulu (1845 to 1893)
    Greatest extent
    About 16 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1890

    Rulers of the Kingdom of Hawaï'i

    • Kamehameha I 1795 to 1819
    • Kamehameha II 1819 to 1824
    • Kamehameha III 1825 to 1854
    • Kamehameha IV 1855 to 1863
    • Kamehameha V 1863 to 1872
    • Lunalilo 1873 to 1874
    • Kalākaua 1874 to 1891
    • Liliʻuokalani 1891 to 1893
