    Majapahit

    1294 to 1518. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1402; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The greatest of the Javanese empires, founded by Raden Wijaya in 1293 after he outwitted the Mongols. Under King Hayam Wuruk and his minister Gajah Mada it claimed lordship over the islands from Sumatra to New Guinea, as Prapanca's poem of 1365 records. Torn by civil war after 1400, it was extinguished by Muslim Demak in the early sixteenth century.

    Government
    Monarchy (Hindu-Javanese kingship)
    Faith
    Shaivism and Buddhism
    Capitals
    Majapahit (Trowulan) (1293 to 1478); Daha (1478 to 1527)
    Greatest extent
    About 911 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1402
