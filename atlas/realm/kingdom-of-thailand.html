    Kingdom of Thailand

    1932 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1932; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Siam under constitutional monarchy from the revolution of 1932, renamed Thailand in 1939. Allied to Japan in the Second World War and to the United States in the Cold War, it has swung between elected governments and military coups, while King Bhumibol, reigning seventy years, made the crown the pivot of national life.

    Government
    Constitutional monarchy, often under military rule
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism
    Capital
    Bangkok (from 1932)
    Greatest extent
    About 513 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1932
