    Illyrian Kingdom

    256 B.C. to 168 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 239 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the Ardiaei on the eastern Adriatic, whose pirate fleets of light galleys preyed on Italian and Greek shipping. Rome made war on Queen Teuta in 229 B.C. and on Demetrius of Pharos in 219, and ended the kingdom by capturing King Genthius at Scodra in 168 B.C.

    • Genthius 180 B.C. to 168 B.C.
