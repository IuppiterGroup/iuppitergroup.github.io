    Sri Lanka

    1972 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1972; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The republic proclaimed in 1972, when Ceylon took the name Sri Lanka; a presidential constitution followed in 1978. From 1983 the island was torn by war between the government and the Tamil Tigers, which ended in 2009, and an economic collapse in 2022 drove President Gotabaya Rajapaksa from office.

    Government
    Unitary semi-presidential republic
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism (given the foremost place); Hindu, Muslim, and Christian minorities
    Capitals
    Colombo (1972 to 1982); Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte (from 1982)
    Greatest extent
    About 66 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1972

    Rulers of the Sri Lanka

    • Sirimavo Bandaranaike (prime minister) 1970 to 1977
    • J. R. Jayewardene (prime minister, then president) 1977 to 1989
    • Ranasinghe Premadasa (president) 1989 to 1993
    • Chandrika Kumaratunga (president) 1994 to 2005
    • Mahinda Rajapaksa (president) 2005 to 2015
    • Maithripala Sirisena (president) 2015 to 2019
    • Gotabaya Rajapaksa (president) 2019 to 2022
    • Ranil Wickremesinghe (president) from 2022
    • Anura Kumara Dissanayake (president) from 2024
