    Khalji Dynasty

    1294 to 1325. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1314; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The second dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate, founded by Jalal al-Din Firuz Khalji in 1290. His nephew Alauddin murdered him, beat back the Mongols, fixed market prices to pay a vast standing army, and through Malik Kafur carried his arms to the far south. The line ended in 1320 when Ghiyath al-Din Tughlaq overthrew the usurper Khusrau Khan.
