    Kingdom of Northumbria

    612 to 935. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 656; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The northernmost Anglo-Saxon kingdom, formed by the union of Bernicia and Deira, from the Humber to the Forth. In the seventh century it was the strongest English realm and, with Lindisfarne, Jarrow, and Bede, a light of Christian learning in the West; the Danes took York in 866, and English lords held out at Bamburgh.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
