    Babylonia

    1894 B.C. to 601 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 615 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The land of Babylon in southern Mesopotamia: the Amorite kingdom of Hammurabi, four centuries of Kassite rule, the Second Dynasty of Isin of Nebuchadnezzar I, and the long struggle against Assyria. Through every change of master the city of Marduk remained the holy and learned capital of the Near East.
