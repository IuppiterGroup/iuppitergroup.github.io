    Huns

    106 to 425. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 396; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The nomad horsemen who came out of the steppes beyond the Volga about A.D. 370, crushed the Alans and the Gothic realm of Ermanaric, and drove the Goths across the Danube into the Roman Empire. Under kings such as Uldin and Rugila they settled on the Hungarian plain and took tribute and service from Rome.

    Government
    Tribal kingship, often shared among brothers
    Faith
    Steppe religion
    Greatest extent
    About 1.5 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 396

    Rulers of the Huns

    • Uldin 400 to 409
    • Charaton 412
    • Octar 420 to 430
    • Rugila 420 to 434
