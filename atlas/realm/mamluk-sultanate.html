    Mamluk Sultanate

    1241 to 1518. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1516; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The state of the Mamluks, Turkish and later Circassian slave soldiers bought as boys and trained to arms, who seized Egypt in 1250. They turned back the Mongols at Ain Jalut, drove the last Crusaders from Syria, and ruled Egypt, Syria, and the Hijaz from Cairo with shadow Abbasid caliphs, until the Ottomans conquered them in 1517.

    Government
    Military oligarchy under an elective sultan
    Faith
    Sunni Islam
    Capital
    Cairo (1250 to 1517)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.9 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1516
