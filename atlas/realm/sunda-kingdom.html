    Sunda Kingdom

    674 to 1578. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 732; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Hindu kingdom of the Sundanese in western Java, which from its capital Pakuan Pajajaran, near Bogor, held the pepper port of Sunda Kalapa. It sought a Portuguese alliance in 1522 against the Muslim coastal states, lost its port to Demak in 1527, and fell to Banten in 1579.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Hinduism and the Sundanese ancestral religion
    Capitals
    Kawali (1333 to 1482); Pakuan Pajajaran (1482 to 1579)
    Greatest extent
    About 115 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 732

    Rulers of the Sunda Kingdom

    • Linggabuana 1350 to 1357
    • Sri Baduga Maharaja 1482 to 1521
    • Surawisesa 1521 to 1535
    • Raga Mulya 1567 to 1579
