    Eastern Moghulistan

    1463 to 1686. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1463; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The eastern realm of the Chagatai khans of Moghulistan, centred on Turfan and the Tarim oases, which parted from the steppe khanate under Ahmad Alaq and his son Mansur Khan and later passed to the khans of Yarkent. Its khans fought the Ming at Hami and the Oirats on the steppe, until the Dzungars ended their line in 1680.
