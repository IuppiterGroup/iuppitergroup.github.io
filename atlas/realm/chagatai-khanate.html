    Chagatai Khanate

    1294 to 1462. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1294; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The khanate of the heirs of Chagatai, second son of Genghis Khan, ruling Transoxiana, the Seven Rivers country, and the Tarim. In the fourteenth century it split: Transoxiana fell to Turkic emirs and at last to Timur, while the eastern khans of Moghulistan, Muslims since Tughlugh Timur, ruled the steppe and the oases into the fifteenth century.
