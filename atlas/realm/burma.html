    Burma

    1820 to 1989. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1822; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Burma from the reign of Bagyidaw to 1989: the Konbaung kingdom, cut down in three wars with Britain until it was annexed in 1886; then a province of British India and from 1937 a separate colony; and from 1948 the independent Union of Burma, under U Nu and, after 1962, the army rule of Ne Win.

    Government
    Monarchy; British colony; parliamentary republic from 1948; military rule from 1962
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism
    Capitals
    Ava (1821 to 1842); Amarapura (1842 to 1859); Mandalay (1859 to 1885); Rangoon (1886 to 1989)
    Greatest extent
    About 791 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1822
