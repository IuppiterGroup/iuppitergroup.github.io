    Kingdom of Bhutan

    1907 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1947; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The hereditary monarchy of the Wangchuck dynasty, founded when an assembly at Punakha chose Ugyen Wangchuck as king in 1907. Long closed to the world, Bhutan opened slowly under its third and fourth kings, made Thimphu its capital, joined the United Nations in 1971, and became a constitutional monarchy with an elected parliament in 2008.

    Government
    Monarchy; constitutional from 2008
    Faith
    Vajrayana Buddhism (Drukpa Kagyu)
    Capitals
    Punakha (1907 to 1955); Thimphu (from 1955)
    Greatest extent
    About 72 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1947

    Rulers of the Kingdom of Bhutan

    • Ugyen Wangchuck 1907 to 1926
    • Jigme Wangchuck 1926 to 1952
    • Jigme Dorji Wangchuck 1952 to 1972
    • Jigme Singye Wangchuck 1972 to 2006
    • Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck from 2006
