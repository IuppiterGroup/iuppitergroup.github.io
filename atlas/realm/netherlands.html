    Netherlands

    1815 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1924; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Kingdom of the Netherlands with its overseas empire: the East Indies, ruled from Batavia, Surinam, and the Antilles. The Indies passed to Indonesia in 1949 and western New Guinea in 1962; Surinam became independent in 1975, while Aruba, Curaçao, and Sint Maarten remain countries within the kingdom.

    Government
    Constitutional monarchy with colonies; parliamentary from 1848
    Faith
    Dutch Reformed Protestantism and Roman Catholicism at home; Islam in the Indies
    Capital
    Amsterdam (from 1813)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.9 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1924

    Rulers of the Netherlands

    • William I (sovereign prince, then king) 1813 to 1840
    • William II 1840 to 1849
    • Johan Rudolph Thorbecke (head of government) 1849 to 1853
    • William III 1849 to 1890
    • Emma (regent) 1890 to 1898
    • Wilhelmina 1890 to 1948
    • Abraham Kuyper (prime minister) 1901 to 1905
    • Hendrikus Colijn (prime minister) 1933 to 1939
    • Pieter Gerbrandy (prime minister in exile) 1940 to 1945
    • Willem Drees (prime minister) 1948 to 1958
    • Juliana 1948 to 1980
    • Beatrix 1980 to 2013
    • Ruud Lubbers (prime minister) 1982 to 1994
    • Wim Kok (prime minister) 1994 to 2002
    • Jan Peter Balkenende (prime minister) 2002 to 2010
    • Mark Rutte (prime minister) from 2010
    • Willem-Alexander from 2013
    • Dick Schoof (prime minister) from 2024
