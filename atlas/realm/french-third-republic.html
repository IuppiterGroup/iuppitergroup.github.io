    French Third Republic

    1871 to 1939. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1920; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The French Republic together with its colonial empire, which grew under the Third Republic from Algeria and Cochinchina to West and Equatorial Africa, Tunisia, Madagascar, Indochina, Morocco, and the Levant mandates, nearly five million square miles ruled from Paris through governors-general and residents.

    Government
    Parliamentary republic with colonies, protectorates, and mandates
    Faith
    Roman Catholicism at home, secular state from 1905; many faiths overseas
    Capitals
    Versailles (1871 to 1879); Paris (1879 to 1940)
    Greatest extent
    About 13 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1920
