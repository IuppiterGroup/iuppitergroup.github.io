    Bahmani Sultanate

    1352 to 1491. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1352; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The first independent Muslim kingdom of the Deccan, founded in 1347 by Ala al-Din Hasan Bahman Shah in revolt against Muhammad bin Tughlaq. Ruling from Gulbarga and then Bidar, it fought Vijayanagara for the Raichur doab; the vizier Mahmud Gawan built up its power and was put to death in 1481. It broke into five sultanates by 1518.
