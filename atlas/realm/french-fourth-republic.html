    French Fourth Republic

    1945 to 1957. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1950; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The French Republic of 1946 to 1958 with its overseas territories, reorganized as the French Union: the departments of Algeria, the federations of West and Equatorial Africa, Madagascar, and the Associated States of Indochina, lost in 1954, while Morocco and Tunisia became independent in 1956.

    Government
    Parliamentary republic with the French Union overseas
    Faith
    Roman Catholicism (secular state); Islam in North and West Africa
    Capital
    Paris (1944 to 1958)
    Greatest extent
    About 13 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1950
