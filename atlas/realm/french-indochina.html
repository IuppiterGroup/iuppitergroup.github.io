    French Indochina

    1860 to 1957. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1927; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The French dominion in Southeast Asia, conquered from Cochinchina in 1859 to Laos in 1893 and federated as the Indochinese Union in 1887; it produced rice, rubber, and coal for France, was occupied by Japan in the Second World War, and was lost in the war against the Viet Minh that ended at Dien Bien Phu in 1954.

    Government
    Colony and protectorates federated under a governor-general
    Faith
    Buddhism, ancestral rites, and Confucian tradition; Roman Catholic missions
    Capitals
    Saigon (1860 to 1902); Hanoi (1902 to 1945); Saigon (1945 to 1954)
    Greatest extent
    About 763 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1927
