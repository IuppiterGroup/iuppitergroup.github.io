    Republic of China

    1912 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1920; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The republic founded after the revolution of 1911, which ruled China through the warlord years from Beijing and from 1928 under the Nationalists from Nanjing. It fought Japan from 1937 to 1945 and lost the civil war to the Communists in 1949, withdrawing to Taiwan, where it became a prosperous democracy.

    Government
    Republic; Nationalist one-party rule 1928 to 1987; democracy from the 1990s
    Faith
    Chinese popular religion, Buddhism, Daoism, and Christianity
    Capitals
    Nanjing (1912); Beijing (1912 to 1928); Nanjing (1928 to 1937); Chongqing (1937 to 1946); Nanjing (1946 to 1949); Taipei (from 1949)
    Greatest extent
    About 9.4 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1920
