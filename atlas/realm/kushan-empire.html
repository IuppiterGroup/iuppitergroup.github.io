    Kushan Empire

    43 to 237. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 127; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of the Kushan clan of the Yuezhi, founded by Kujula Kadphises and reaching at its height under Kanishka from the Oxus to the Ganges. Astride the Silk Road between Rome, Parthia, and China, it saw the art of Gandhara and Mathura, the first images of the Buddha, and the passage of Buddhism into Central Asia and China.

    Government
    Monarchy (King of Kings)
    Faith
    Iranian cults, Buddhism, and Hinduism
    Capital
    Purushapura (127 to 237)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.3 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 127
