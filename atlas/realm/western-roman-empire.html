    Western Roman Empire

    395 to 479. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 396; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The western half of the Roman Empire after 395, ruled from Milan and then Ravenna by emperors more and more in the hands of their barbarian generals. It lost Britain, Spain, Africa, and Gaul to Goths, Vandals, Franks, and others, and ended when Odoacer deposed Romulus Augustulus in 476.

    Government
    Autocratic monarchy (emperor)
    Faith
    Christianity (Catholic)
    Capitals
    Milan (395 to 402); Ravenna (402 to 476)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.1 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 396
