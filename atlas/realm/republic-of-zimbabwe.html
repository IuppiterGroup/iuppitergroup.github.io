    Republic of Zimbabwe

    1980 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1980; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The former Rhodesia, independent under majority rule in 1980 and ruled by Robert Mugabe, whose Fifth Brigade massacred thousands of Ndebele in the Gukurahundi of the 1980s and whose seizure of white-owned farms from 2000 brought economic collapse and hyperinflation. The army removed him in 2017.

    Government
    Presidential republic (dominant party)
    Faith
    Christianity; traditional religion
    Capitals
    Salisbury (1980 to 1982); Harare (from 1982)
    Greatest extent
    About 394 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1980

    Rulers of the Republic of Zimbabwe

    • Canaan Banana (president) 1980 to 1987
    • Robert Mugabe 1980 to 2017
    • Emmerson Mnangagwa from 2017
