    Judea

    144 B.C. to 5. Shown above as it stood in 77 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Jewish state won from the Seleucids by the Maccabees and ruled by their Hasmonean heirs as high priests and then kings, who conquered Idumea, Samaria, and Galilee. Pompey made it a client of Rome in 63 B.C., and Herod the Great, king by Rome's grace, rebuilt the Temple on a grand scale.

    Government
    High priests and kings, then a Roman client kingdom
    Faith
    Judaism
    Capital
