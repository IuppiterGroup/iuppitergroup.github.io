    Songhai Empire

    1463 to 1608. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1564; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of the Songhai of Gao on the Niger bend, raised by Sunni Ali, who took Timbuktu in 1468 and Djenné in 1473, and organized by Askia Muhammad into the largest state West Africa had known. Moroccan musketeers shattered it at Tondibi in 1591; its last kings held out in Dendi downriver.

    Government
    Monarchy (the sunni, then the askia)
    Faith
    Islam, with Songhai traditional religion
    Capital
    Gao (1464 to 1591)
    Greatest extent
    About 670 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1564

    Rulers of the Songhai Empire

    • Sunni Ali 1464 to 1492
    • Sunni Baru 1492 to 1493
    • Askia Muhammad I 1493 to 1528
    • Askia Musa 1528 to 1531
    • Askia Ishaq I 1539 to 1549
    • Askia Dawud 1549 to 1582
    • Askia Ishaq II 1588 to 1591
    • Askia Nuh 1591 to 1599
