    Kingdom of Spain

    1516 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1890; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The monarchy of the united crowns of Castile and Aragon, which under Charles V and Philip II ruled the Netherlands, half of Italy, and the Americas, and was the great Catholic power of Europe. It lost its primacy to France in the seventeenth century, passed to the Bourbons in 1700, and endured through Napoleon, republics, and civil war.

    Government
    Monarchy (absolute, then constitutional); republics in 1873 and from 1931 to 1939
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity
    Capitals
    Toledo (1516 to 1561); Madrid (1561 to 1601); Valladolid (1601 to 1606); Madrid (from 1606)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.2 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1890
