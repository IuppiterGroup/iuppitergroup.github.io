    Khmer Empire

    800 to 1430. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1206; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of Angkor, founded by Jayavarman II in 802, which at its height under Suryavarman II and Jayavarman VII ruled from the Mekong delta to the Khorat plateau and the Malay isthmus. Remembered for Angkor Wat, the Bayon, and the great reservoirs that fed its rice fields, it declined after 1300 and lost Angkor to Ayutthaya in 1431.

    Government
    Divine kingship (devaraja)
    Faith
    Hinduism (Shaivism and Vaishnavism) and Mahayana Buddhism, then Theravada Buddhism
    Capitals
    Hariharalaya (802 to 889); Yasodharapura (889 to 928); Koh Ker (928 to 944); Yasodharapura (944 to 1431)
    Greatest extent
    About 944 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1206
