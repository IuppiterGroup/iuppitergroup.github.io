    Grand Duchy of Kraków

    1279 to 1293. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1285; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The senior duchy of Kraków, seat of the princeps among the Polish dukes, held by Leszek the Black and then by Henry IV Probus of Silesia. In 1291 it fell to Wenceslaus II of Bohemia.
