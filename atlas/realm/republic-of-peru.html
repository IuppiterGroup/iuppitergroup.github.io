    Republic of Peru

    1822 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1822; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The republic proclaimed by San Martín at Lima in 1821 and secured at Ayacucho in 1824, the old heart of Spain's South American empire. Through decades of caudillos it lived on guano, lost its southern provinces to Chile in the War of the Pacific, and passed in the twentieth century between oligarchs, generals, and reformers.

    Government
