    Latin Empire

    1206 to 1261. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1220; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire set up in Constantinople by the knights of the Fourth Crusade and the Venetians after they stormed and sacked the city in 1204. Poor, quarrelsome, and hemmed in by Bulgarians and Greeks, it shrank to the city and its environs, until a Nicaean force entered it almost unopposed in 1261.
