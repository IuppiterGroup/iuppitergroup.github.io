    Khanate of Khiva

    1512 to 1919. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1857; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The khanate of Khwarazm on the lower Amu Darya, founded in 1511 by the Arabshahid Uzbeks and ruled from Khiva from the seventeenth century. A land of oases and slave markets where Persian and Russian captives were sold, it was taken by Nader Shah in 1740, passed to the Qongrat dynasty in 1804, and was a Russian protectorate from 1873 until the Bolsheviks ended it in 1920.
