    Mexico

    1868 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1868; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Mexican republic restored by Benito Juárez after the fall of Maximilian, ruled for more than thirty years by Porfirio Díaz, convulsed by the Revolution of 1910 to 1920, and governed for seventy-one years by the party that became the Institutional Revolutionary Party, until Vicente Fox won in 2000. Lázaro Cárdenas nationalized its oil in 1938.

    Government
    Federal presidential republic
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity; a secular state
    Capital
    Mexico City (from 1867)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.0 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1868
