    Wei

    409 B.C. to 204 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 400 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    One of the three states carved from Jin, ruled from Anyi and then from Daliang, the later Kaifeng, which made it known also as Liang. Under Marquis Wen and his minister Li Kui it was the first great power of the Warring States, until Qi broke it at Guiling and Maling. Qin flooded Daliang and destroyed it in 225 B.C.

    Government
    Marquisate, then kingdom
    Faith
    Ancestor worship
    Capitals
    Anyi (403 B.C. to 362 B.C.); Daliang (361 B.C. to 225 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 111 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 400 B.C.
