    Old Kingdom of Egypt

    2686 B.C. to 2161 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 2600 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Egypt of the Third to Eighth Dynasties, ruled from Memphis: the age of Djoser's Step Pyramid, the pyramids of Giza, the sun temples of Ra, and the Pyramid Texts. After the long reign of Pepi II the governors of the provinces grew too strong and the central state broke apart.

    Government
    Divine monarchy (pharaoh)
    Faith
    Egyptian religion (Ra, Ptah, Horus)
    Capital
    Memphis (2686 B.C. to 2161 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 67 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 2600 B.C.
