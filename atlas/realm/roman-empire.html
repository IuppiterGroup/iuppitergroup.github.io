    Roman Empire

    31 B.C. to 394. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 117; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of the Caesars from Augustus to Theodosius I, master of the whole Mediterranean, from Britain and the Rhine to Egypt and the Euphrates, the Roman Peace of law, roads, and cities. It survived the crisis of the third century, became Christian under Constantine, and was divided between the sons of Theodosius in 395.

    Government
    Monarchy (principate, then dominate)
    Faith
    Roman religion, then Christianity from 380
    Capitals
    Rome (27 B.C. to 286); Nicomedia (286 to 324); Milan (286 to 395); Constantinople (330 to 395)
    Greatest extent
    About 5.3 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 117
