    Parthian Empire

    239 B.C. to 237. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 161; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Iranian empire of the Arsacid kings, horsemen from the steppe east of the Caspian who took Iran and Mesopotamia from the Seleucids. Its mounted archers destroyed Crassus at Carrhae, and for nearly five centuries it was the rival of Rome in the East, until the Persian Ardashir overthrew it in A.D. 224.

    Government
    Feudal monarchy (King of Kings) with great noble houses
    Faith
    Zoroastrianism and Iranian religion, with Greek and Babylonian cults
    Capitals
    Hecatompylos (217 B.C. to 129 B.C.); Ctesiphon (129 B.C. to 224)
    Greatest extent
    About 3.0 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 161
