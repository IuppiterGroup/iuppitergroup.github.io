    Later Tang

    926 to 935. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 926; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The second of the Five Dynasties, proclaimed in 923 by the Shatuo prince Li Cunxu, who destroyed the Later Liang and conquered Former Shu. A lover of actors who was killed in a mutiny in 926, he was followed by the able Li Siyuan; the dynasty fell in 936 when Shi Jingtang brought in the Khitan.

    Government
    Imperial military monarchy
    Faith
    Buddhism and Chinese popular religion
    Capital
    Luoyang (923 to 936)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.5 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 926

    Rulers of the Later Tang

    • Emperor Zhuangzong of Later Tang (Li Cunxu) 923 to 926
    • Emperor Mingzong of Later Tang (Li Siyuan) 926 to 933
    • Li Conghou 933 to 934
    • Li Congke 934 to 936
