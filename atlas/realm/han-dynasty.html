    Han Dynasty

    202 B.C. to 220. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 91; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The dynasty founded by Liu Bang in 202 B.C., which ruled China, with the interruption of Wang Mang, for four centuries. It made Confucianism the creed of the state, drove back the Xiongnu, opened the Silk Road, and saw the making of paper; the Chinese still call themselves the people of Han.

    Government
    Imperial monarchy with a Confucian bureaucracy
    Faith
    Confucian state cults and Daoism; Buddhism from the first century A.D.
    Capitals
    Chang'an (202 B.C. to 9); Luoyang (25 to 190); Chang'an (190 to 195); Xu (196 to 220)
    Greatest extent
    About 4.5 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 91
