    Democratic Republic of Vietnam

    1956 to 1975. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1973; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Communist state proclaimed by Ho Chi Minh in Hanoi on 2 September 1945, which fought the French to victory at Dien Bien Phu, ruled the north after the division at Geneva in 1954, and waged the long war against the South and the United States until the fall of Saigon in 1975.

    Government
    Communist one-party state
    Faith
    State atheism; Buddhism and Catholicism among the people
    Capital
    Hanoi (1945 to 1976)
    Greatest extent
    About 181 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1973

    Rulers of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam

    • Ho Chi Minh (president) 1945 to 1969
    • Truong Chinh (general secretary) 1951 to 1956
    • Pham Van Dong (premier) 1955 to 1976
    • Le Duan (first secretary) 1960 to 1976
    • Ton Duc Thang (president) 1969 to 1976
