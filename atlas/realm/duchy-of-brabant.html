    Duchy of Brabant

    1260 to 1430. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1415; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The rich duchy of the Low Countries about Brussels, Leuven, and Antwerp, whose dukes won Limburg at Worringen in 1288 and swore to respect the liberties of their towns in the Joyous Entry of 1356. After the death of Duchess Joanna it passed to a younger branch of Burgundy, and in 1430 to Philip the Good.

    Government
    Feudal duchy
    Faith
