    Twenty-third Dynasty of Egypt

    800 B.C. to 701 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 800 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    A rival line of Libyan kings of the eighth century B.C., seated by Manetho's tradition at Leontopolis in the Delta, though its kings held Thebes and many scholars now place their seat in Upper Egypt. Its most notable king, Osorkon III, made his daughter Shepenupet God's Wife of Amun.

    Government
    Monarchy (pharaoh)
    Faith
    Egyptian religion (Amun)
    Capital
    Leontopolis (818 B.C. to 715 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 65 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 800 B.C.

    Rulers of the Twenty-third Dynasty of Egypt

    • Pedubast I 818 B.C. to 793 B.C.

    • Takelot III 764 B.C. to 757 B.C.
    • Rudamun 757 B.C. to 754 B.C.
    • Iuput II 754 B.C. to 715 B.C.
