    Argentine Republic

    1861 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1885; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Argentine nation reunited under Buenos Aires after Pavón in 1861, which by immigration from Italy and Spain, by railways, and by the wheat and beef of the pampas became one of the richest nations on earth by 1914. Its twentieth century brought coups, Peronism, the Dirty War of the military junta, and defeat in the Falklands in 1982.
