    Kievan Rus'

    882 to 1240. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1034; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The realm of the Rurikid princes along the Dnieper and the Volkhov, from Novgorod to Kiev, built on the river trade from the Varangians to the Greeks. Christianized under Vladimir in 988 and given law and cathedrals by Yaroslav the Wise, it splintered among quarreling princes after 1132 and was destroyed by the Mongols in 1237 to 1240.

    Government
    Monarchy of the Rurikid house, succession by seniority
    Faith
    Slavic paganism, then Orthodox Christianity from 988
    Capital
    Kiev (882 to 1240)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.2 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1034
