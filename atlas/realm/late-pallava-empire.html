    Late Pallava Empire

    283 to 897. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 306; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Pallava kings of Kanchipuram, masters of the northern Tamil country and rivals of the Chalukyas and Pandyas. Mahendravarman I and Narasimhavarman I, conqueror of Vatapi, cut the first rock temples of the south and the reliefs and rathas of Mamallapuram, and their script carried Indian letters to Southeast Asia. The Cholas overthrew them about 897.

    Government
