    Goryeo

    922 to 1394. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1375; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Korean kingdom founded by Wang Geon in 918, which united the peninsula in 936 and ruled it from Kaesong for nearly five centuries; from its name comes Korea. It beat back the Khitan, endured a century of military dictators and thirty years of Mongol invasion, carved the Tripitaka Koreana, and made celadon of unrivalled beauty.

    Government
    Monarchy; military rule 1170 to 1270; Mongol vassal from 1270
    Faith
    Buddhism and Confucian learning
    Capitals
    Kaesong (919 to 1232); Ganghwa (1232 to 1270); Kaesong (1270 to 1392)
    Greatest extent
    About 195 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1375

    Rulers of the Goryeo

    • Taejo of Goryeo (Wang Geon) 918 to 943
    • Gwangjong of Goryeo 949 to 975
    • Seongjong of Goryeo 981 to 997
    • Hyeonjong of Goryeo 1009 to 1031
    • Munjong of Goryeo 1046 to 1083
    • Choe Chung-heon (military dictator) 1196 to 1219
    • Gongmin of Goryeo 1351 to 1374
    • Gongyang of Goryeo 1389 to 1392
