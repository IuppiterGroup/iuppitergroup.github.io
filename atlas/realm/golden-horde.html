    Golden Horde

    1294 to 1501. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1294; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The western realm of the Mongols, the ulus of Genghis Khan's eldest son Jochi, founded by Batu after the conquest of the Rus and the Kipchak steppe. From Sarai on the Volga its khans, Muslims from Özbeg's time, took tribute from the Rus princes for two centuries; Timur ruined it, and it broke into the khanates of Kazan, Crimea, and Astrakhan.
