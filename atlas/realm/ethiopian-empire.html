    Ethiopian Empire

    1272 to 1935. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1908; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Christian empire of the Ethiopian highlands under the Solomonic dynasty, which traced its line to Solomon and the Queen of Sheba. It withstood the jihad of Ahmad Gragn, the Jesuit missions, and the Era of the Princes, and under Menelik II crushed Italy at Adwa in 1896, remaining free until Mussolini's invasion of 1935.

    Government
    Monarchy (negusa nagast, king of kings)
    Faith
    Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity
    Capitals
    Roving royal camp (1270 to 1635); Gondar (1636 to 1855); Debre Tabor (1855 to 1868); Mekelle (1872 to 1889); Addis Ababa (1889 to 1936)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.1 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1908
