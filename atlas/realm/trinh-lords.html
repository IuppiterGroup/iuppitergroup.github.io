    Trịnh lords

    1572 to 1787. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1775; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The house of Trinh, which ruled northern Vietnam in the name of the powerless Le emperors from the 1540s to 1786. From Thang Long the Trinh lords fought the Mac and then the Nguyen lords of the south in a fifty-year war; the Tay Son swept them away in 1786.

    Government
    Hereditary military regency under nominal Le emperors
    Faith
    Neo-Confucianism and Buddhism
    Capitals
    Thanh Hoa (1545 to 1592); Thang Long (1592 to 1787)
    Greatest extent
    About 148 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1775

    Rulers of the Trịnh lords

    • Trinh Kiem 1545 to 1570
    • Trinh Tung 1570 to 1623
    • Trinh Trang 1623 to 1657
    • Trinh Tac 1657 to 1682
    • Trinh Can 1682 to 1709
    • Trinh Cuong 1709 to 1729
    • Trinh Doanh 1740 to 1767
    • Trinh Sam 1767 to 1782
