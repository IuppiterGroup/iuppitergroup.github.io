    Holy Roman Empire

    962 to 1259. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1453; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of the German kings, revived by Otto I in 962 and later styled Holy and Roman, embracing Germany, Bohemia, the Low Countries, Burgundy, and northern Italy. Its emperors contended with the popes and then with their own princes, until by 1648 it was a loose federation of hundreds of states; Francis II dissolved it in 1806.

    Government
    Elective monarchy (seven electors from 1356)
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity; Lutheran and Calvinist states recognized in 1555 and 1648
    Capitals
    Prague (1346 to 1400); Vienna (1556 to 1583); Prague (1583 to 1612); Vienna (1612 to 1806)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.1 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1453
