    Visigothic Kingdom

    426 to 723. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 490; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the Visigoths, settled in Aquitaine in 418 with its capital at Toulouse, driven into Spain by Clovis in 507, and ruled at last from Toledo. Catholic from Reccared's conversion in 587, it gave Spain its law and its great church councils, until the Arab and Berber invasion of 711 destroyed it.

    Government
    Elective monarchy with church councils
    Faith
    Arian Christianity, then Catholic from 587
    Capitals
    Toulouse (418 to 507); Barcelona (507 to 554); Toledo (554 to 711)
    Greatest extent
    About 697 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 490
