    Kingdom of Mrauk U

    1429 to 1787. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1534; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Arakanese kingdom founded in 1430 by Min Saw Mon, who regained his throne with help from the sultan of Bengal. Its Buddhist kings took Muslim titles, held Chittagong for over a century, and with Portuguese freebooters raided Bengal for slaves. Mrauk U, its temple-studded capital, fell to the Burmese king Bodawpaya in 1785.
