    Roman Republic

    509 B.C. to 32 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 36 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The commonwealth of the Roman people from the expulsion of the kings to the rise of Augustus, governed by annual consuls, the Senate, and the assemblies. Its legions conquered Italy, Carthage, the Hellenistic East, and Gaul, until the civil wars of Marius and Sulla, Pompey and Caesar destroyed it.
