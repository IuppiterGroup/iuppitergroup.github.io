    Abbasid Caliphate

    750 to 1259. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 778; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The caliphate of the house of al-Abbas, the Prophet's uncle, which overthrew the Umayyads in 750 and ruled from its new capital of Baghdad. Under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun it was the center of the world's learning; from 945 its caliphs were wards of Buyid and Seljuk lords, until the Mongols sacked Baghdad in 1258.

    Government
    Hereditary caliphate
    Faith
    Sunni Islam
    Capitals
    Kufa (750 to 752); Anbar (752 to 762); Baghdad (762 to 836); Samarra (836 to 892); Baghdad (892 to 1258)
    Greatest extent
