    Western Satraps

    43 to 489. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 270; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Saka satraps of western India, the Kshaharatas of Nahapana and the long line of Chashtana, who ruled Gujarat, Malwa, and Sind from Ujjain for some three centuries. Rudradaman left the first long Sanskrit inscription at Junagadh, and their dated silver coins give an unbroken chronology. Chandragupta II of the Guptas destroyed them about A.D. 400.
