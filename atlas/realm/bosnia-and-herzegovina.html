    Bosnia and Herzegovina

    1992 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 2006; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The republic of Bosniaks, Serbs, and Croats that declared independence in March 1992 and was torn by three and a half years of war, with the siege of Sarajevo and the massacre at Srebrenica; the Dayton agreement of 1995 divided it into a Bosniak-Croat Federation and a Serb Republic under an international High Representative.

    Government
    Federal state of two entities with a three-member presidency and an international High Representative
    Faith
    Islam, Serbian Orthodoxy, and Roman Catholicism
    Capital
    Sarajevo (from 1992)
    Greatest extent
    About 52 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 2006

    Rulers of the Bosnia and Herzegovina

    • Alija Izetbegović (president of the presidency) 1990 to 1996
    • Carl Bildt (high representative) 1995 to 1997
    • Carlos Westendorp (high representative) 1997 to 1999
    • Wolfgang Petritsch (high representative) 1999 to 2002
    • Paddy Ashdown (high representative) 2002 to 2006
    • Valentin Inzko (high representative) 2009 to 2021
    • Christian Schmidt (high representative) from 2021
