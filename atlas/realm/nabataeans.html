    Nabataeans

    164 B.C. to 105. Shown above as it stood in 126 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Arab kingdom of the Nabataeans, desert traders in incense and spices who carved their capital Petra from the rose-red cliffs, mastered the storing of water, and held the caravan roads from the Hejaz to Damascus. Its kings were clients of Rome until Trajan annexed the realm as the province of Arabia in A.D. 106.
