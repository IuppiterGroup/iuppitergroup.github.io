    Xia

    410 to 438. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 426; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The state of Helian Bobo, a Xiongnu warlord of the Tiefu clan, who built the white-walled fortress city of Tongwan on the edge of the Ordos, its walls tested, the story goes, by driving in an awl and killing the builders wherever it went in. He took Chang'an in 418; Northern Wei destroyed his state by 431.

    Government
    Imperial monarchy
    Faith
    Steppe religion and Buddhism
    Capital
    Tongwan (418 to 427)
    Greatest extent
    About 230 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 426

    Rulers of the Xia

    • Helian Bobo 407 to 425
    • Helian Chang 425 to 428
    • Helian Ding 428 to 431
