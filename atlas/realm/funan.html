    Funan

    51 to 640. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 546; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The first great state of mainland Southeast Asia, on the lower Mekong and its delta, known chiefly from Chinese envoys. Its port of Oc Eo traded with India, China, and Rome, and its kings, heirs of the Kaundinya legend, took Indian names, letters, and gods, until Chenla absorbed it in the sixth and seventh centuries.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Hinduism (Shaiva and Vaishnava) and Buddhism
    Capitals
