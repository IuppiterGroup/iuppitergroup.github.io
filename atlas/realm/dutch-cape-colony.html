    Dutch Cape Colony

    1653 to 1795. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1788; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Dutch East India Company's settlement at the Cape of Good Hope, founded by Jan van Riebeeck in 1652 to refresh the Indies fleets. Its free burghers, Huguenot refugees, and slaves from Madagascar and the East were forebears of the Afrikaners and the Cape Coloured; its trekboers pushed east to the Great Fish River. Britain seized it in 1795.

    Government
    Company colony under a governor and Council of Policy
    Faith
