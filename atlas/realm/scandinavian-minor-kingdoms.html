    Scandinavian minor kingdoms

    500 to 1002. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 825; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The petty kingdoms and chieftaincies of Scandinavia in the Migration and Viking ages, among the Danes, the Geats, the Svear, and the peoples of Norway, before the united kingdoms of Denmark, Norway, and Sweden. Their kings raised the Danevirke, raided the Frankish coasts, and received the first Christian missionaries.
