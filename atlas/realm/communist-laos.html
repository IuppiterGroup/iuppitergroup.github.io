    Communist Laos

    1976 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1976; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Lao People's Democratic Republic, proclaimed by the Pathet Lao on 2 December 1975 after the abdication of King Savang Vatthana. A close ally of Vietnam, it sent thousands to re-education camps and drove much of the Hmong people into exile, and since 1986 has opened slowly to markets and to Chinese investment.

    Government
    Communist one-party state
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism and spirit worship
    Capital
    Vientiane (from 1975)
    Greatest extent
    About 230 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1976

    Rulers of the Communist Laos

    • Souphanouvong (president) 1975 to 1991
    • Kaysone Phomvihane (prime minister, then president) 1975 to 1992
    • Nouhak Phoumsavanh (president) 1992 to 1998
    • Khamtai Siphandon (president) 1998 to 2006
    • Choummaly Sayasone (president) 2006 to 2016
    • Bounnhang Vorachith (president) 2016 to 2021
    • Thongloun Sisoulith (president) from 2021
