    Dominican Republic

    1846 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1865; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The eastern two-thirds of Hispaniola, which won independence from Haiti in 1844 under Juan Pablo Duarte's Trinitarios, returned briefly to Spanish rule from 1861 to 1865, and was occupied by the United States from 1916 to 1924. Rafael Trujillo ruled it as his private estate from 1930 until his assassination in 1961.

    Government
