    New Zealand

    1907 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1908; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The self-governing colony, from 1907 a dominion, of the islands of Aotearoa, where British settlers and the Maori lived under the Treaty of Waitangi. A pioneer of votes for women and of the welfare state, it fought at Gallipoli and in both world wars, and since the 1980s has been known for refusing nuclear arms and redressing Maori grievances.

    Government
    Parliamentary constitutional monarchy
    Faith
    Christianity, with growing irreligion; the Maori Ratana and Ringatu churches
    Capital
    Wellington (from 1865)
    Greatest extent
    About 266 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1908
