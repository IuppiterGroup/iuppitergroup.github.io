    Kingdom of Norway

    1814 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1932; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Norway, ceded by Denmark to Sweden in 1814, gave itself the Eidsvoll constitution that May and entered a personal union under the Swedish king; it dissolved the union in 1905 and chose Haakon VII, endured German occupation from 1940 to 1945, and became a founder of NATO and, through North Sea oil, one of the richest nations on earth.

    Government
    Constitutional monarchy; parliamentary government from 1884
    Faith
    Lutheran Christianity (Church of Norway)
    Capitals
    Christiania (1814 to 1924); Oslo (from 1925)
    Greatest extent
    About 420 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1932
