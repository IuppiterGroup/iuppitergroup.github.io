    Mughal Empire

    1529 to 1857. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1700; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire founded by Babur at Panipat in 1526, which under Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, and Aurangzeb ruled nearly the whole subcontinent with a Persianate court, a ranked nobility, and a revenue system of great refinement, and left the Taj Mahal and the Red Fort. After 1707 it decayed, and the British deposed its last emperor in 1858.

    Government
    Monarchy (padishah)
    Faith
    Sunni Islam, with Akbar's eclectic court; Hindu majority
    Capitals
    Agra (1526 to 1571); Fatehpur Sikri (1571 to 1585); Lahore (1585 to 1598); Agra (1598 to 1648); Delhi (Shahjahanabad) (1648 to 1857)
    Greatest extent
    About 3.7 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1700
