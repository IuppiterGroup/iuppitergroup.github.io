    Principality of Moldavia

    1363 to 1858. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1600; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The principality between the eastern Carpathians and the Dniester, founded when Bogdan of Maramureș threw off Hungarian rule in the 1360s. Stephen the Great held it against Turks, Hungarians, and Poles in a reign of forty-seven years, but from the sixteenth century it paid tribute to the sultan, and its princes were Phanariot Greeks after 1711.

    Government
    Principality; Ottoman vassal
    Faith
