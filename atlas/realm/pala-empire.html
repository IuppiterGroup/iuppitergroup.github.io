    Pala Empire

    750 to 1168. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 825; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Buddhist dynasty of Bengal and Bihar founded about 750 by Gopala, chosen king to end anarchy. Dharmapala and Devapala made it a great power contending for Kannauj, and the Palas endowed Nalanda, Vikramashila, and Somapura, whence Buddhism passed to Tibet. Ramapala revived it after the revolt of the Kaivartas; the Senas supplanted it in the twelfth century.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Mahayana and Vajrayana Buddhism; Hindu subjects
    Capitals

    Greatest extent
