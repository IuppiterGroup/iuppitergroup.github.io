    Samanid Empire

    886 to 999. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 911; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    A Persian dynasty descended from a Zoroastrian landowner of Balkh, which ruled Transoxiana and Khorasan from Bukhara as Abbasid governors in name and sovereigns in fact. Under it Persian rose again as a language of letters, with the poets Rudaki and Daqiqi and the young Avicenna; Turkish generals and Karakhanid invaders brought it down in 999.

    Government
    Hereditary emirate under nominal Abbasid suzerainty
    Faith
    Sunni Islam
    Capitals
    Samarkand (875 to 892); Bukhara (892 to 999)
    Greatest extent
    About 3.1 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 911
