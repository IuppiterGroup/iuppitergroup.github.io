    Russian-occupied Ukraine

    2014 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 2024; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Ukrainian lands held by Russia: the Crimea annexed in March 2014, the self-styled people's republics of Donetsk and Luhansk set up by Russian-backed forces that spring, and the territory seized in the invasion of 2022, four provinces of which Moscow declared annexed in September 2022 in violation of international law.

    Government
    Military occupation and annexation by the Russian Federation, through installed administrations
    Faith
    Orthodox Christianity
    Capitals
    Simferopol (from 2014); Donetsk (from 2014); Luhansk (from 2014)
    Greatest extent
    About 102 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 2024
