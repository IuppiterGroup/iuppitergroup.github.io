    Byzantine Empire

    633 to 1453. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 633; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Roman Empire in the East, Greek in tongue and Orthodox in faith, ruled from Constantinople for over a thousand years. It held the Arabs at its walls, brought the Slavs to Christianity, kept the learning of antiquity, and outlived the sack of 1204, until the Ottoman Turks stormed its capital in 1453.

    Government
    Autocratic monarchy
    Faith
    Orthodox Christianity
    Capitals
    Constantinople (633 to 1204); Nicaea (1204 to 1261); Constantinople (1261 to 1453)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.4 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 633
