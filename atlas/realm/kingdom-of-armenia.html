    Kingdom of Armenia

    326 B.C. to 428. Shown above as it stood in 77 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the Armenian highlands, ruled by the Orontids, the Artaxiads, and from the first century A.D. by a branch of the Arsacids. Under Tigranes the Great it was briefly an empire from the Caspian to Syria; caught ever after between Rome and Persia, it became the first Christian kingdom, until Persia ended the monarchy in 428.

    Government
    Hereditary monarchy with great noble houses (nakharars)
    Faith
    Armenian paganism and Zoroastrianism, then Christianity from about 301
    Capitals
    Armavir (326 B.C. to 210 B.C.); Yervandashat (210 B.C. to 176 B.C.); Artaxata (176 B.C. to 77 B.C.); Tigranocerta (77 B.C. to 69 B.C.); Artaxata (69 B.C. to 120); Vagharshapat (120 to 336); Dvin (336 to 428)
    Greatest extent
    About 526 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 77 B.C.
