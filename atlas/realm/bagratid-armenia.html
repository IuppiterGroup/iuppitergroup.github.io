    Bagratid Armenia

    886 to 1045. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 886; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The restored kingdom of Armenia under the Bagratid house, recognized by caliph and emperor alike in the 880s. From Ani, its capital from 961 and the city of a thousand and one churches, its kings ruled a land of trade and building, until Byzantium annexed it in 1045 on the eve of the Seljuk conquest.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Armenian Apostolic Christianity
    Capitals
    Bagaran (885 to 890); Shirakavan (890 to 929); Kars (929 to 961); Ani (961 to 1045)
    Greatest extent
    About 99 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 886

    • Gagik II 1042 to 1045
