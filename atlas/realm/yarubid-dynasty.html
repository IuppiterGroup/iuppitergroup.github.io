    Yarubid dynasty

    1626 to 1743. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1718; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Ya'rubi imams of Oman, elected Ibadi rulers who united the tribes from 1624, expelled the Portuguese from Muscat in 1650, and built a sea empire reaching to Mombasa and Zanzibar. Their great forts at Nizwa, Rustaq, and Jabrin still stand; a civil war over the imamate in the 1720s brought in the Persians, and the Al Bu Said took power in the 1740s.

    Government
    Imamate, hereditary in practice
    Faith
    Ibadi Islam
    Capital

    Greatest extent

    • Sultan ibn Saif II 1711 to 1718
