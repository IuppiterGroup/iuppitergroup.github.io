    Electorate of Saxony

    1363 to 1805. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1648; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The electorate of Saxony on the middle Elbe, held after 1423 by the Wettins, lords of the silver mines of the Erzgebirge. Frederick the Wise sheltered Luther, Maurice won the electorate for the Albertine line in 1547, and Augustus the Strong, King of Poland, made Dresden the Florence of the Elbe.

    Government
    Electorate
    Faith
