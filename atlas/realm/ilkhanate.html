    Ilkhanate

    1294 to 1343. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1294; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Mongol khanate of Persia founded by Hulagu, grandson of Genghis Khan, who destroyed the Assassins and the Abbasid caliphate between 1256 and 1258. Its khans warred with the Mamluks and the Golden Horde; Ghazan made Islam its faith in 1295, and it left great works of history and building before dissolving after 1335.

    Government
    Khanate under the nominal suzerainty of the Great Khan
    Faith
    Tengrism and Buddhism, then Islam from 1295
    Capitals
    Maragheh (1256 to 1265); Tabriz (1265 to 1306); Soltaniyeh (1306 to 1335)
    Greatest extent
    About 3.9 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1294
