    Kashmiri Sultanate

    1344 to 1587. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1344; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Muslim sultanate of the Vale of Kashmir, founded by Shah Mir in 1339 after the death of the last Hindu queen, Kota Rani. Under Sultan Sikandar the temples were thrown down and Islam spread by force and by the preaching of Persian Sufis; his son Zain-ul-Abidin restored tolerance and the arts. Akbar annexed the valley in 1586.
