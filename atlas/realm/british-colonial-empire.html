    British Colonial Empire

    1706 to 1999. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1898; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Britain's overseas colonies and dependencies outside India: the West Indian sugar islands, Canada, the Australian colonies, the Cape, and later most of tropical Africa and the Pacific, governed through the Colonial Office from 1854; independence came to most after 1957, and the return of Hong Kong in 1997 closed the imperial age.

    Government
    Crown colonies, protectorates, and self-governing dominions under the Crown and Parliament
    Faith
    Protestant Christianity among settlers; many faiths among subject peoples
    Capital
    London (1706 to 1999)
    Greatest extent
    About 9.2 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1898
