    Sultanate of Harar

    1547 to 1578. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1547; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The remnant of the Adal state after Gragn's fall, ruled from the walled city of Harar, whose Jugol wall was raised by the emir Nur ibn Mujahid, husband of Gragn's widow, who slew Emperor Gelawdewos in 1559. Worn down by the Oromo migrations, Adal's rulers withdrew to Aussa in 1577.
