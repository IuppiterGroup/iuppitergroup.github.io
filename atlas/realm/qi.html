    Qi

    750 B.C. to 204 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 286 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The rich state of the Shandong peninsula, mistress of salt and iron, whose Duke Huan was the first hegemon. In the fourth century B.C. the Tian family took its throne, and under the Tian kings Linzi was the greatest city of the age. Qi was the last state to fall to Qin, and revived briefly after Qin's collapse.

    Government
    Feudal duchy, then kingdom
    Faith
    Worship of Heaven and the ancestors; the Eight Spirits of Qi
    Capital
    Linzi (750 B.C. to 221 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 206 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 286 B.C.

    • King Min of Qi 300 B.C. to 284 B.C.
    • Tian Jian, King of Qi 264 B.C. to 221 B.C.
    • Tian Rong, King of Qi 206 B.C. to 205 B.C.
