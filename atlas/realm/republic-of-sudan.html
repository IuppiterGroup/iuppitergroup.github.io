    Republic of Sudan

    1956 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1992; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The largest country of Africa until 2011, independent of Anglo-Egyptian rule in 1956 and torn between its Arab and Muslim north and its African south by civil wars from 1955 to 1972 and 1983 to 2005. Omar al-Bashir's Islamist regime waged war in Darfur; the south seceded in 2011, and a new civil war broke out in 2023.

    Government
    Republic, mostly under military rule
    Faith
    Sunni Islam; Christianity and traditional religions in the south
    Capital
    Khartoum (from 1956)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.5 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1992
