    Kingdom of Sweden

    980 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1721; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the Swedes and Götar about Lake Mälaren and the lakes of the south, Christianized from the eleventh century, which conquered Finland and then joined the Kalmar Union. Gustav Vasa freed it in 1523; under Gustavus Adolphus and his heirs it became the great power of the North, and after 1721 it sank back to a lesser rank under the rule of its estates.

    Government
    Monarchy (elective, hereditary from 1544)
    Faith
    Norse paganism, then Catholic, then Lutheran Christianity from 1527
    Capitals
    Sigtuna (980 to 1187); Stockholm (from 1252)
    Greatest extent
    About 781 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1721
