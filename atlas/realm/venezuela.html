    Venezuela

    1834 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1853; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The republic that left Gran Colombia in 1830 under José Antonio Páez, ruled through the nineteenth century by caudillos from Páez and the Monagas brothers to Antonio Guzmán Blanco. Under the dictator Juan Vicente Gómez it became the world's foremost oil exporter; democracy came in 1958, and Hugo Chávez's Bolivarian revolution in 1999.

    Government
    Federal presidential republic
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity
    Capital

    Rulers of the Venezuela

    • José Antonio Páez 1830 to 1835
    • José Tadeo Monagas 1847 to 1851
    • Antonio Guzmán Blanco 1870 to 1877
    • Juan Vicente Gómez 1908 to 1935
    • Marcos Pérez Jiménez 1952 to 1958
    • Rómulo Betancourt 1959 to 1964
    • Hugo Chávez 1999 to 2013
    • Nicolás Maduro from 2013
