    Mauretania

    212 B.C. to 42. Shown above as it stood in 27 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the Moors in northwest Africa, from the Atlantic to the river Mulucha, ruled by kings such as Bocchus I, who betrayed Jugurtha to Sulla, and Juba II, the scholar king of Iol Caesarea. Caligula had its last king, Ptolemy, put to death in A.D. 40, and Claudius made it two provinces.

    Government
    Monarchy (Roman client)
    Faith
    Berber and Punic religion; Roman cults
    Capital
    Iol Caesarea (25 B.C. to 40)
    Greatest extent
    About 219 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 27 B.C.

    Rulers of the Mauretania

    • Bocchus I 110 B.C. to 80 B.C.
    • Bogud 49 B.C. to 38 B.C.
    • Bocchus II 49 B.C. to 33 B.C.
    • Juba II 25 B.C. to 23
    • Ptolemy of Mauretania 23 to 40
