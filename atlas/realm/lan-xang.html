    Lan Xang

    1363 to 1708. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1375; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Lao kingdom of the Million Elephants, founded by Fa Ngum in 1353 and stretching along the middle Mekong from the borders of Yunnan to Champasak. Its capitals of Luang Prabang and Vientiane were great Buddhist centers; it reached its height under Sourigna Vongsa in the seventeenth century, and after his death split into three kingdoms between 1707 and 1713.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism and the worship of spirits (phi)
    Capitals
    Xieng Thong (Luang Prabang) (1353 to 1560); Vientiane (1560 to 1707)
    Greatest extent
    About 379 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1375
