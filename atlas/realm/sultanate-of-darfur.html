    Sultanate of Darfur

    1609 to 1876. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1612; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The sultanate of the Keira dynasty of the Fur in the mountains of Jebel Marra and the plains of western Sudan, which grew rich on the Forty Days Road to Egypt, trading slaves, ivory, and ostrich feathers. Al-Zubayr Pasha conquered it for Egypt in 1874; restored under Ali Dinar in 1898, it was annexed by the British in 1916.
