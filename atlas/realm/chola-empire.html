    Chola Empire

    850 to 1259. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1028; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire of the imperial Cholas of Thanjavur, founded by Vijayalaya about 848. Rajaraja I and Rajendra I conquered southern India and Lanka, sent armies to the Ganges and a fleet against Srivijaya, raised the great temples of Thanjavur and Gangaikonda Cholapuram, and governed through self-ruling village assemblies. The Pandyas and Hoysalas reduced it in the thirteenth century.
