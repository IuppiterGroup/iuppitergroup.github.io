    Rouran Khaganate

    402 to 554. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 407; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The steppe empire that ruled Mongolia between the Xianbei and the Türks, whose rulers first made khagan the title of a steppe emperor. They fought the Northern Wei for a century and a half; in 552 their Türk ironsmiths rose against them, and the last Rouran fled west, perhaps to become the Avars of Europe.

    Government
