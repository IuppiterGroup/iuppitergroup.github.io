    Ayutthaya Kingdom

    1352 to 1768. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1600; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Siamese kingdom founded by U Thong in 1351 on an island in the Chao Phraya, heir to Khmer kingship and Thai custom. For four centuries it was among the great powers and ports of Southeast Asia, trading with China, Japan, Persia, and Europe and twice falling to Burma, until the Burmese destroyed the city in 1767.

    Government
    Absolute monarchy with the sakdina hierarchy of ranks
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism with Brahmanical court rites
    Capital
    Ayutthaya (1351 to 1767)
    Greatest extent
    About 439 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1600
