    Emirate of Sicily

    850 to 1093. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 878; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Muslim Sicily, won from the Eastern Roman Empire over three-quarters of a century from 827 and ruled first by Aghlabid governors from Palermo, then from 948 by the Kalbid emirs under the Fatimids. Citrus, sugar cane, and cotton flourished and Palermo grew into a great city, until feuding emirs called in the Normans, who won the island by 1091.
