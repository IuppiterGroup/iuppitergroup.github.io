    German Empire

    1871 to 1918. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1918; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The federal empire of twenty-five German states under the King of Prussia, proclaimed at Versailles in 1871 after Bismarck's wars; the leading industrial and military power of the Continent, it built a navy to rival Britain's, went to war in 1914 beside Austria-Hungary, and fell in the revolution of November 1918.

    Government
    Federal constitutional monarchy under the King of Prussia as German Emperor
    Faith
    Protestant majority (Lutheran and Reformed), large Roman Catholic minority
    Capital
    Berlin (1871 to 1918)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.4 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1918
