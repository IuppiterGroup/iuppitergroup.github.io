    Ptolemaic Kingdom

    307 B.C. to 28 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 31 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Macedonian kingdom of Egypt founded by Ptolemy I, whose house ruled from Alexandria as pharaohs to the Egyptians and as kings to the Greeks, with the Museum and the Library at its capital. Weakened by native revolts and family wars, it ended with the death of Cleopatra VII in 30 B.C.

    Government
    Hellenistic monarchy in pharaonic form
    Faith
    Greek and Egyptian religion (Serapis); cult of the kings
    Capitals
    Memphis (323 B.C. to 311 B.C.); Alexandria (311 B.C. to 30 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.3 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 31 B.C.
