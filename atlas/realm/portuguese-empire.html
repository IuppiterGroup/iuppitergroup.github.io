    Portuguese Empire

    1502 to 1823. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1820; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    Portugal's seaborne empire as a whole, the first of the European world empires and the last to end: forts and colonies from Brazil and the Gold Coast to Angola, Mozambique, Goa, Malacca, Macau, and Timor. Brazil, its richest part, became independent in 1822; the African colonies were held until 1975.

    Government
    Colonial empire of the Portuguese crown
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity
    Capitals
    Lisbon (1502 to 1808); Rio de Janeiro (1808 to 1821); Lisbon (1821 to 1910)
    Greatest extent
    About 8.2 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1820
