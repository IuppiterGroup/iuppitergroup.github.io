    Dutch East Indies

    1800 to 1949. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1915; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Dutch colonial empire in the Indonesian archipelago, which passed from the Company to the state in 1800 and, after British rule from 1811 to 1816, spread by war and treaty over the whole archipelago. It drew vast wealth from coffee, sugar, tin, rubber, and oil until Japan overran it in 1942; the Dutch yielded sovereignty in 1949.

    Government
    Colonial governor-generalship under the Netherlands
    Faith
    Islam among most subjects; Christianity, Hinduism, and others
    Capital
    Batavia (1800 to 1949)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.9 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1915
