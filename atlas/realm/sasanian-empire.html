    Sasanian Empire

    215 to 643. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 623; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The second Persian empire, founded by Ardashir I and ruled from Ctesiphon by Kings of Kings who made Zoroastrianism the faith of the realm. For four centuries it fought Rome on equal terms, capturing Valerian and sacking Antioch, until the Arab conquests destroyed it after Qadisiyyah and Nahavand.

    Government
    Absolute monarchy (King of Kings) with great noble houses
    Faith
    Zoroastrianism
    Capital
    Ctesiphon (226 to 637)
    Greatest extent
    About 5.0 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 623
