    Taebong

    911 to 921. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 911; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom proclaimed in central Korea by Gung Ye, a disowned Silla prince turned monk and rebel, first as Later Goguryeo in 901 and from 911 as Taebong, with its capital at Cheorwon. He called himself the living Maitreya and grew so cruel that his generals overthrew him in 918 and made Wang Geon king.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Buddhism; cult of Maitreya
    Capitals
    Songak (901 to 905); Cheorwon (905 to 918)
    Greatest extent
    About 76 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 911

    • Gung Ye 901 to 918
