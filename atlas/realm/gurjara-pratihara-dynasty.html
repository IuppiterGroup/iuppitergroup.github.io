    Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty

    732 to 1039. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 783; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Rajput imperial house that turned back the Arabs of Sind in the eighth century and from Kannauj ruled most of northern India in the ninth. The Arab Sulaiman judged its cavalry the finest in India and its king the sternest foe of the Arabs. Worn down by the Rashtrakutas, it was shattered by Mahmud of Ghazni in 1018.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Hinduism (Vaishnava and Shaiva)
    Capitals
    Ujjain (730 to 816); Kannauj (816 to 1018); Bari (1018 to 1036)
    Greatest extent
