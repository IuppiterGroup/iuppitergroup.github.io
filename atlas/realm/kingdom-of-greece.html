    Kingdom of Greece

    1832 to 1966. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1920; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom created by the powers in 1832 for the Bavarian prince Otto and given in 1863 to the Danish George I; it grew by the Ionian Islands, Thessaly, Crete, and Macedonia, was riven by the quarrel of Constantine I and Venizelos, and was a republic from 1924 to 1935 before the crown returned.

    Government
    Constitutional monarchy; dictatorship under Metaxas 1936 to 1941
    Faith
    Greek Orthodox Christianity
    Capitals
    Nauplia (1832 to 1834); Athens (1834 to 1973)
    Greatest extent
    About 214 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1920

    Rulers of the Kingdom of Greece

    • Otto 1832 to 1862
    • George I 1863 to 1913
    • Eleftherios Venizelos (prime minister) 1910 to 1915
    • Constantine I 1913 to 1917
    • Alexander 1917 to 1920
    • Constantine I 1920 to 1922
    • George II 1922 to 1924
    • George II 1935 to 1947
    • Ioannis Metaxas (dictator) 1936 to 1941
    • Paul 1947 to 1964
    • Constantine II 1964 to 1973
