    Duchy of Bavaria

    1260 to 1622. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1352; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The duchy of the Wittelsbach house from 1180, long divided among quarreling branches at Munich, Landshut, Ingolstadt, and Straubing until Albert IV reunited it and decreed primogeniture in 1506. Its dukes led the Catholic cause in Germany, and Maximilian I, head of the Catholic League, won the electoral dignity in 1623.
