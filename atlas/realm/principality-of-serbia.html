    Principality of Serbia

    768 to 1879. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 922; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Serbian principality of the early Middle Ages in the hills of Raška, ruled by the house of Vlastimir between Byzantium and Bulgaria; and the modern principality won from the Ottomans in the risings of Karađorđe in 1804 and Miloš Obrenović in 1815, autonomous from 1830 and independent in 1878.
