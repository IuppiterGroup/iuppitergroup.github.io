    Kingdom of Cambodia

    1431 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1475; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Khmer kingdom after the abandonment of Angkor, ruled from Phnom Penh, Longvek, and Oudong and squeezed for four centuries between Siam and Vietnam. A French protectorate from 1863, it won independence under Norodom Sihanouk in 1953, suffered war and the Khmer Rouge after 1970, and was restored as a constitutional monarchy in 1993.

    Government
    Monarchy (absolute, then constitutional from 1947 and again from 1993)
    Faith
    Theravada Buddhism
    Capitals
    Phnom Penh (1434 to 1463); Longvek (1528 to 1594); Oudong (1618 to 1866); Phnom Penh (from 1866)
    Greatest extent
    About 281 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1475
