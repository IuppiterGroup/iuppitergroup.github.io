    Kingdom of Alamannia

    462 to 731. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 476; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The land of the Alamanni, a league of Germanic tribes on the upper Rhine and Danube who had raided Gaul and Italy since the third century. Defeated by Clovis about 496 and brought under Frankish rule, they were governed by their own dukes until the Carolingian mayors crushed them at Cannstatt in 746.

    Government
    Tribal kingship, then a duchy under the Franks
    Faith
    Germanic paganism, then Christianity
    Greatest extent
    About 111 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 476

    Rulers of the Kingdom of Alamannia

    • Leuthari 553 to 554
    • Butilin 553 to 554
    • Lantfrid 709 to 730
