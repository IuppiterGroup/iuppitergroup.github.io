    Korean Empire

    1898 to 1910. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1908; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The empire proclaimed by King Gojong in 1897 to assert Korea's equality with China and Japan, which built railways, telegraphs, and electric trams in Seoul. Japan, victorious over Russia, made it a protectorate in 1905, forced Gojong to abdicate in 1907, and annexed it in 1910.

    Government
    Absolute monarchy
    Faith
    Neo-Confucianism, Buddhism, Cheondogyo, and Christianity
    Capital
    Hanseong (1897 to 1910)
    Greatest extent
    About 219 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1908

    Rulers of the Korean Empire

    • Gwangmu Emperor (Gojong) 1897 to 1907
    • Yunghui Emperor (Sunjong) 1907 to 1910
