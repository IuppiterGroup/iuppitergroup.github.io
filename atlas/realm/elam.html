    Elam

    3200 B.C. to 601 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 1150 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The ancient kingdom of southwestern Iran, joining the lowland city of Susa with the highland realm of Anshan, with its own tongue and scripts. For two thousand years the Elamites traded and warred with Mesopotamia, sacked Ur and plundered Babylonia, carrying off Hammurabi's laws, until Ashurbanipal laid Susa waste about 646 B.C.

    Government
    Monarchy, often with fraternal succession
    Faith
    Elamite religion (Inshushinak, Humban, Kiririsha)
    Capital
    Susa (3200 B.C. to 646 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 214 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 1150 B.C.
