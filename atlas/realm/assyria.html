    Assyria

    2025 B.C. to 901 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 1150 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The kingdom of the god Ashur on the upper Tigris: a merchant city-state under the Old Assyrian kings, the heart of Shamshi-Adad's realm, then a vassal of Mitanni. From Ashur-uballit I the Middle Assyrian kings made it a great military power, which reached the Mediterranean under Tiglath-Pileser I.

    Government
    Monarchy (the king as vice-regent of Ashur)
    Faith
    Assyrian religion (Ashur)
    Capitals
    Assur (1808 B.C. to 1233 B.C.); Kar-Tukulti-Ninurta (1233 B.C. to 1207 B.C.); Assur (1207 B.C. to 911 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 517 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 1150 B.C.
