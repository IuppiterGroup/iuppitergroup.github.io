    Uruk

    3400 B.C. to 1803 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 2116 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The greatest city of early Sumer, where writing began in the precinct of Inanna and Gilgamesh ruled in legend. Lugalzagesi governed all Sumer from it before Sargon; Utu-hengal of Uruk drove out the Gutians; and under its own kings it stood apart again in the 19th century B.C.

    Government
    Monarchy (city-state)
    Faith
    Sumerian religion (An and Inanna)
    Capital
    Uruk (3400 B.C. to 1803 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 26 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 2116 B.C.

    Rulers of the Uruk

    • Lugal-zage-si 2358 B.C. to 2334 B.C.
    • Utu-hengal 2119 B.C. to 2113 B.C.
