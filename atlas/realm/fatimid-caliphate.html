    Fatimid Caliphate

    911 to 1176. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1015; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Isma'ili Shi'ite caliphate founded in Ifriqiya in 909 by Ubayd Allah al-Mahdi, descended by its own reckoning from Fatima, which ruled from Cairo, its own foundation, from 973. At its height it held Egypt, Syria, the Hijaz, and Sicily and rivaled Baghdad; worn by famine, schism, and the Crusades, it was ended by Saladin in 1171.

    Government
    Hereditary Isma'ili imamate-caliphate
    Faith
    Isma'ili Shi'a Islam
    Capitals
    Raqqada (909 to 921); al-Mahdiyya (921 to 948); al-Mansuriyya (948 to 973); Cairo (973 to 1171)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.0 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1015
