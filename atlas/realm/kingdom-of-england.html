    Kingdom of England

    936 to 1706. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1706; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The realm of the English crown with its lands beyond England: Normandy and the Angevin inheritance in France, the lordship and later kingdom of Ireland, Wales, and the Channel Islands. Its kings lost their French empire by 1453 save Calais, broke with Rome under Henry VIII, and in 1603 shared their king with Scotland.

    Government
    Monarchy, limited by Parliament
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity, then the Church of England from 1534
    Capitals
    Winchester (936 to 1066); London (1066 to 1708)
    Greatest extent
    About 430 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1706
