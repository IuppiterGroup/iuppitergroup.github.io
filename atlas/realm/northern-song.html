    Northern Song

    961 to 1138. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1056; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Song dynasty while it ruled from Kaifeng, from Zhao Kuangyin's accession in 960 to the Jurchen capture of the capital in 1127. It was an age of scholar-officials chosen by examination, of printing, paper money, gunpowder, and the compass, and of a commercial economy unmatched in the world, though it never won back the north from the Liao.

    Government
    Imperial monarchy with a civil bureaucracy
    Faith
    Buddhism, Daoism, and Neo-Confucianism
    Capital
    Kaifeng (960 to 1127)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.8 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1056
