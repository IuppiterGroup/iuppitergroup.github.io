    Serbian Despotate

    1402 to 1458. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1422; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Serbian state of the despots, a title granted by the Eastern Roman emperor to Stefan Lazarević in 1402, which revived in the shadow of the Ottomans. Stefan made Belgrade his capital and a center of letters; Đurađ Branković built the great fortress of Smederevo on the Danube, which fell to Mehmed II in 1459.

    Government
    Despotate, often an Ottoman vassal
    Faith
    Orthodox Christianity
    Capitals
    Belgrade (1405 to 1427); Smederevo (1430 to 1459)
    Greatest extent
    About 74 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1422

    Rulers of the Serbian Despotate

    • Stefan Lazarević 1402 to 1427
    • Đurađ Branković 1427 to 1456
    • Lazar Branković 1456 to 1458
