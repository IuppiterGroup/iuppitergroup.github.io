    Shunga Empire

    170 B.C. to 67 B.C.. Shown above as it stood in 91 B.C.; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The dynasty founded by the Brahmin general Pushyamitra Shunga, who slew the last Maurya about 185 B.C. Ruling the Ganges heartland from Pataliputra and Vidisha, the Shungas fought the Indo-Greeks and Kalinga and revived the Vedic horse sacrifice, while the stupas of Bharhut and Sanchi were raised. The last king, Devabhuti, was murdered by his minister Vasudeva Kanva.

    Government
    Monarchy
    Faith
    Vedic Hinduism (Brahmanical revival); Buddhism flourished
    Capital
    Pataliputra (185 B.C. to 73 B.C.)
    Greatest extent
    About 1.2 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for 91 B.C.
