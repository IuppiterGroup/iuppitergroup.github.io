    County of Savoy

    1260 to 1415. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1402; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Alpine county astride the passes between France and Italy, held since the eleventh century by the heirs of Humbert White-Hands. Peter II, the little Charlemagne, won Vaud; the Green Count, Amadeus VI, crusaded against the Turks and took Gallipoli in 1366; and in 1416 the emperor made Amadeus VIII a duke.
