    Merovingian Empire

    710 to 717. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 710; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Frankish realm of the Merovingian house, sprung by legend from Merovech, from Childeric I and Clovis to the shadow kings whom the mayors of the palace governed. Divided again and again into Austrasia, Neustria, and Burgundy, it embraced nearly all of Gaul and much of Germany.

    Government
    Hereditary monarchy, divided among heirs
    Faith
    Catholic Christianity
    Capitals
    Tournai (457 to 486); Soissons (486 to 508); Paris (508 to 717)
    Greatest extent
    About 734 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 710
