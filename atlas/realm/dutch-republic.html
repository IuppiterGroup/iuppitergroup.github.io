    Dutch Republic

    1582 to 1795. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1769; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The East Indies dominion of the Dutch Republic, ruled from Batavia by the Dutch East India Company (VOC), chartered in 1602. The Company won a monopoly of the Moluccan spices by war and treaty, held the coasts of Java and the chief ports of the archipelago, and became overlord of Java's princes before its dissolution in 1799.

    Government
    Chartered company rule (VOC) under the States General
    Faith
    Dutch Reformed Church among the Dutch; Islam and other faiths among their subjects
    Capitals
    Banten (1603 to 1610); Ambon (1610 to 1619); Batavia (1619 to 1799)
    Greatest extent
    About 296 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1769
