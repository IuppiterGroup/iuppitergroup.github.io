    Republic of Iraq

    2005 to today. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 2018; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The federal republic of Iraq under the constitution of 2005, governed by Shi'ite-led coalitions with a Kurdish president, a Sunni speaker, and an autonomous Kurdistan in the north. It lost a third of its land to the Islamic State in 2014 and won it back by 2017 with American air power and Iranian-backed militias.

    Government
    Federal parliamentary republic
    Faith
    Shi'a Islam majority, with Sunni and Christian minorities
    Capital
    Baghdad (from 2005)
    Greatest extent
    About 404 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 2018
