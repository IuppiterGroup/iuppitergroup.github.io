    First Bulgarian Empire

    682 to 1017. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 850; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The Bulgar khanate south of the Danube, founded by Asparukh in 681, which fused Turkic Bulgars and Slavs into one people. Under Krum and Simeon it rivaled Byzantium; Boris took the Christian faith, and the schools of Preslav and Ohrid made Slavonic a written tongue. Basil II conquered it by 1018.

    Government
    Khanate, then tsardom
    Faith
    Tengrism, then Orthodox Christianity from 864
    Capitals
    Pliska (681 to 893); Preslav (893 to 971); Ohrid (997 to 1018)
    Greatest extent
    About 432 thousand sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 850
