    Kazakh Khanate

    1468 to 1847. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1595; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    The khanate of the Kazakhs, Jochid nomads who broke away from the Uzbeks under Kerey and Janibek and spread over the steppe from the Caspian to the Altai. Divided into the Great, Middle, and Junior Hordes and ravaged by the Dzungars, its khans sought Russian protection from 1731; Russia abolished the khanships in the 1820s and crushed Kenesary's rising in 1847.

    Government
    Khanate, elective among Jochid sultans
    Faith
    Sunni Islam with steppe traditions
    Capital
    Turkestan (1598 to 1723)
    Greatest extent
    About 2.7 million sq. km, as drawn on this atlas for A.D. 1595
