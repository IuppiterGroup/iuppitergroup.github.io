    Zengid dynasty

    1139 to 1259. Shown above as it stood in A.D. 1169; drag the timeline to watch it rise and fall.

    A Turkish dynasty founded by Imad al-Din Zengi, atabeg of Mosul, who took Edessa from the Franks in 1144. His son Nur al-Din united Muslim Syria, preached the holy war, and sent Shirkuh and Saladin to Egypt; after his death Saladin took Syria, and Zengid princes lingered at Mosul until 1234 and in Sinjar and the Jazira.

    Government
    Hereditary atabegates
    Faith
    Sunni Islam
    Capitals
    Mosul (1127 to 1234); Aleppo (1128 to 1183); Damascus (1154 to 1174)
    Greatest extent
