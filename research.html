Dividend Study

An empirical study of 5,106 ex-dividend events across 80 S&P 500 stocks (2010 to 2025). On average, prices fall by about 81% of the dividend at the open, not the full amount. Full bibliography included.

Finance Research

RSI Study

A backtest of four RSI strategies across 100 U.S. stocks from 1962 to 2026. Oversold signals produced positive average trades, but every strategy underperformed buy-and-hold because capital sits idle between signals, and the stock universe carries survivorship bias. Full bibliography included.
