Financial Modeling

DCF Modeler

Value a stock on EPS or free cash flow per share, with Bear, Base, and Bull scenarios and a Monte Carlo range. Runs in your browser. No Excel required.

Free

DCF Modeler is a single self-contained HTML file: no install, no accounts, no cloud. Enter a ticker, price, shares outstanding, and the current EPS or FCF per share, then set growth, growth decay, discount rate, exit multiple, and buyback assumptions. It projects per-share earnings or cash flow, discounts them, adds an exit-multiple terminal value, and shows Bear, Base, and Bull values side by side.

A 2,000-run Monte Carlo varies growth, discount rate, exit multiple, and buybacks around your base case to show the range of outcomes, and a sensitivity table shows how value moves with the key inputs. It's free. If it helps you, consider supporting our work.

Audio Production

Vox Codex

Turn a PDF into a narrated audiobook. Automatic chapter detection, 15 natural-sounding voices, and MP3 export.

Free

Vox Codex is a Windows desktop app that turns PDFs into narrated audio. Open a PDF and it extracts the text and detects chapters, which you can review and edit. Pick one of 15 English neural voices (US, UK, Australian, and Irish), adjust rate and pitch, preview a sample, and export each chapter as an MP3 plus one combined audiobook file.

Narration uses Microsoft's Edge neural voices through the free edge-tts service, so it needs an internet connection and your text is sent to Microsoft to generate the audio. No API key, account, or subscription is needed. The program takes a moment to initialize on first launch.

Portfolio Management · Free

Portfolio Tracker & Stock Screener

Bloomberg-style portfolio analytics on your local machine. Real-time P&L, sector allocation, full financials, and a built-in S&P 500 screener with 12 filter criteria.

Free

Portfolio Tracker runs locally via a lightweight Python web server — open your browser and you have a full institutional-style portfolio dashboard. Track unlimited positions with real-time P&L, cost basis, unrealized gains, sector allocation breakdowns, and interactive performance charts pulled live from Yahoo Finance.

The built-in S&P 500 stock screener lets you filter the entire index by 12 criteria simultaneously — P/E ratio, market cap, dividend yield, beta, profit margins, debt-to-equity, revenue growth, 52-week proximity, and more. Click any result to load full financial statements, income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow data inline. All data is fetched on-demand via yfinance. No accounts, no subscriptions, no cloud.
